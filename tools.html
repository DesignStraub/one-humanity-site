LifeBar

By Sunday you have no idea where the week went. An ESP32-S3 bar display that turns invested minutes into EXP, levels and earned titles. It sits on the desk and can’t be swiped away. All data stays on the device.

  1. Idea: a resource bar like in a strategy game
  2. First board, printed case, firmware running
  3. Running on my own desk — level 14 and counting
  4. Next: firmware published, build guide, parts list
